Why every idea needs two sources

The reporting gap

Official national figures undercount. For road deaths in 2021 the WHO estimate and the government's own reported total differ by close to three times — so each idea in the catalog pairs a national source with an independent or international one, and the model gets calibrated against the discrepancy instead of inheriting it.
